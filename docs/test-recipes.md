# Test recipes

Two sample recipes for manually testing Milestone 1. Each has the three fields the
recipe form asks for: **Title**, **Image URL** and **Ingredients** (one per row).

---

## 1. Margherita Pizza

**Title:** Margherita Pizza

**Image URL:** https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1565299624946-b28f40a0ae38?w=1200

**Ingredients:**

- 1 lb pizza dough
- 1/2 cup crushed San Marzano tomatoes
- 8 oz fresh mozzarella, torn
- 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 clove garlic, minced
- 1/2 tsp sea salt
- 8 fresh basil leaves

## 2. Rainbow Power Bowl

**Title:** Rainbow Power Bowl

**Image URL:** https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1512621776951-a57141f2eefd?w=1200

**Ingredients:**

- 1 cup cooked quinoa
- 1 can chickpeas, drained and roasted
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 1 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 small sweet potato, cubed and roasted
- 1 cup shredded red cabbage
- 2 tbsp tahini
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 handful microgreens
